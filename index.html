Notes

  • Each span between two poles can only be entered once (Pole X → Pole Y and Pole Y → Pole X are treated as the same span).
  • Each pole can have one or more destination poles.
  • Meter numbers must be exactly 6 alphanumeric characters (letters and/or digits, e.g. 550189 or AB1234).
  • Duplicate meter numbers are not allowed.
  • Data is saved locally on this device until exported or deleted.
